Core Technology

From Foot Pressure to Clinical Insights

An end-to-end framework transforming high-frequency mechanical force into actionable biomechanical intelligence.

Continuous Kinematic Sensing

Ultra-thin, medically calibrated smart insoles integrate seamlessly into everyday footwear mapping spatial force distribution.

Edge-Computed Metrics

Onboard microprocessor pipelines filter signal noise in real time, executing local spatial algorithms to extract critical diagnostic markers.

Predictive Care Biofeedback

Cloud analytics track balance degradation while machine learning models identify micro-hesitations to mitigate gait freezing.

Targeted Applications

Built for Complex Movement Challenges

👣

Foot Drop Support

Provides real-time biofeedback and gait tracking to assist clearance and toe drag prevention.

⚡

Parkinson's Gait Freezing

Detects movement hesitation early and delivers cues to unfreeze natural gait cycles.

🛡️

Elderly Fall Prediction

Identifies subtle balance degradation trajectories before stumbles or fall incidents occur.

📊

Clinical Gait Indexing

Generates high-precision objective metrics validated against medical laboratory force plates.
